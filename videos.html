Common Posture Mistakes

⚠️

Rounded Back Deadlift

Keep your spine neutral. Engage your lats before pulling. Think "chest up, hips back."

⚠️

Bouncing Bench Press

Control the eccentric. Pause briefly at the chest. Never bounce the bar off your sternum.

⚠️

Knees Caving In

Push knees out during squats and lunges. Strengthen your abductors. Use cues like "spread the floor."

⚠️

Ego Lifting

Use weight you can control through full ROM. Quality reps > heavy weight with bad form. Always.
