Common Questions

Is it really free?

100% free. No hidden costs, no card required, no obligation to join. We want you to experience BeFit and decide for yourself.

What should I bring?

Just bring yourself, comfortable workout clothes, and a pair of training shoes. We provide water, towels, and everything else you need.

How long is the trial session?

The full experience takes about 90 minutes — including the tour, assessment, and your personal training session.

Can I bring a friend?

Absolutely! Bring a friend and you both get a free trial session. Training with a partner is one of the best ways to stay consistent.

Do I need to be fit to start?

Not at all. We welcome all fitness levels — from complete beginners to advanced athletes. Our coaches adapt the session to your current ability.
